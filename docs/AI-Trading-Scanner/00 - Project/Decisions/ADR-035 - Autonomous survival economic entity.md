# ADR-035 — Autonomous survival economic entity

- Status: ACCEPTED as a future experiment architecture; Agent 5 and the 4+1 runner are not implemented or activated.
- Date: 2026-09-17.

## Context

A survival-only objective can be gamed by holding cash forever. A minimum trade count can instead force destructive overtrading or meaningless tiny orders. A self-organizing participant can also create capital accidentally if descendants are modeled as independent funded agents, and unconstrained adaptive/model output could cross deterministic world and authority boundaries.

## Decision

Define Agent 5 as one autonomous survival economic entity with one initial virtual-capital contribution and no later deposits, refills or resurrection. Descendant allocations are exclusive internal claims against that entity, reconciled atomically by an engine-owned coordinator. Descendants never create buying power or separate top-level capital.

Evaluate a lexicographic active-survival objective over finite preregistered epochs: boundary compliance, active survival duration, economic value/growth, then drawdown/cost/turnover efficiency. A deterministic engine-owned causal opportunity reference and risk/time-weighted material participation rule prevent permanent-cash and token-trade gaming while retaining first-class NO_TRADE. Persistent inactivity is a distinct `INACTIVE_FAILURE`, not fabricated financial death.

Freeze the objective, opportunity/activity/death rules, initial policy, permitted mutation schemas, capability envelope and update algorithm before the run. Agent 5 may causally evolve policy state, roles and internal allocations only inside that envelope. Every accepted/rejected change is immutable evidence. Roles are declarative and executable only through registered primitives; self-organization is not source-code self-modification.

Economic state is ALIVE, AT_RISK or irreversibly DEAD based on conservative causally marked net liquidation value and a registered execution-viability/ruin methodology. Death removes all descendant authority immediately. Remaining exposure becomes an engine-owned estate resolved under deterministic rules; proceeds cannot resurrect the entity.

Future model output is untrusted proposal evidence. Replay uses the captured model envelope and deterministic validation rather than calling the model again. Models cannot mutate the ledger, scheduler, world rules, source, authority or connectivity.

## Rationale

Finite opportunity-aware epochs make inactivity observable without rewarding raw trade volume. A single economic entity gives capital conservation one enforceable root. A frozen adaptation envelope satisfies reproducibility while allowing causal organizational evolution. Separating nondeterministic reasoning from deterministic economic application preserves auditability.

## Consequences

Agent 5 is intentionally not equivalent to the four fixed strategies and cannot be inserted into Experiment 1. A 4+1 study is a separate system-level comparison. Numeric activity, materiality, ruin and opportunity-reference values remain open and must be preregistered before execution. The reference can introduce bias, so results must include its version and sensitivity limitations.

Agent 5 requires durable policy/entity journals, adversarial capital-conservation and boundary tests, and independent review before any serious historical run. This ADR adds no AI provider, broker, PAPER or LIVE authority.

## Alternatives considered

Unlimited cash inactivity; arbitrary minimum trade counts; a single weighted reward that allows compensation between death risk and activity; separately funded descendants; unrestricted generated code; mutable objectives; and cash-only death.

## Conditions for revisiting

Revisit only through a new policy/experiment version after simulation evidence identifies gaming, reference bias or a better viability/death methodology. Retain prior policies, failed organizations and results.
