# ADR-001 — US equities and ETFs first

- Status: ACCEPTED as initial architecture policy; implementation/market performance remain unvalidated.
- Date: 2026-09-13.

## Context

The initial investigation needs a bounded market and consistent currency/session model.

## Decision

Start with US equities/ETFs and the declared convenience development universe.

## Rationale

A bounded scope makes data quality, accounting and execution assumptions inspectable.

## Consequences

No claim of optimal universe; effective membership/actions and selection limits must be recorded.

## Alternatives considered

Crypto, FX, European equities and broad multi-asset support.

## Conditions for revisiting

Revisit only with a registered research need, coverage and capability evidence.
