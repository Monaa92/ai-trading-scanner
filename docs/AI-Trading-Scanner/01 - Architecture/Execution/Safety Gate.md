# Final deterministic safety gate

Required on every executable entry and management command, independent of approval policy and AI. Pipeline: available market data → deterministic strategy → optional AI → risk → safety preflight → approval policy → fresh risk/safety revalidation → atomic intent/reservation/audit → dispatch revalidation → adapter. See [[01 - Architecture/Execution/Approval Workflow]]. Safety is an explicit domain/application policy boundary, not a second independent sizing formula.

| Check | Authoritative owner | Safety-gate responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Equity, cash/FX, quantity, monetary/percentage risk, stop/target geometry, net RR | Risk engine under versioned agent and account profiles | Require fresh PASS bound to identical proposed terms and input/config revisions; never recompute differently |
| Daily loss, maximum positions/trades, reserved exposure and buying power | Risk/account coordinator, scoped agent and parent account | Require capacity and no applicable lock; atomic reservation rejects races |
| Strategy eligibility/AI policy | Strategy and validated selection layer | Require still-valid candidate/mandate; AI cannot revive a strategy rejection |
| Data/run mode, execution environment, submission mode, approval policy, owner/allocation/experiment membership | Administrative configuration and registry | Reject wrong endpoint/account, disabled trading, unauthorized ORDER_ENABLED/FULL_AUTO/LIVE, inactive/locked participant, changed frozen hash |
| Data/FX/account freshness, session/cutoff/halt | Data/calendar/account health services; financial consequence remains risk-owned | Reject stale/missing evidence, invalid session or clock; no stale approval may proceed |
| Supported instrument/order/protection, healthy broker | Capability and broker-health adapters | Require verified unexpired capability/connection/reconciliation; reject unsupported or unknown |
| Duplicate/conflicting orders/positions | Intent ledger and account coordinator | Require unique identities, owned position/exit quantity and no unresolved submission or netting conflict |
| Approval/expiry/management mandate | Approval service and frozen config | Require exact consent or explicit FULL_AUTO mandate under ORDER_ENABLED; SIGNAL_ONLY has no submission capability |
| Durability/single writer | Persistence/outbox and lease services | Require committed records, live fencing token and fresh bounded dispatch lease |

Result: immutable SafetyEvaluation with rule-level results, reason codes, scope, proposal hash, risk decision ID, permission/config/allocation revisions, broker/data-health snapshots, as_of and valid_until. Reasons include WRONG_ENVIRONMENT, TRADING_DISABLED, STALE_DATA, INVALID_SESSION, DAILY_LOCKOUT, MAX_POSITIONS, MAX_TRADES, RISK_EXCEEDED, INSUFFICIENT_BUYING_POWER, INVALID_STOP, INVALID_TARGET, INSUFFICIENT_RR, DUPLICATE_ORDER, POSITION_CONFLICT, BROKER_UNHEALTHY, STALE_APPROVAL, UNSUPPORTED_SYMBOL and ACCOUNT_INCONSISTENT. Risk-owned failures retain their RiskDecision IDs even when safety blocks dispatch. Logs distinguish primary blocking gate from additional reasons; counts must not confuse one rejected candidate with many failed checks.

Preflight is explanatory, not a reusable authority token. In SIGNAL_ONLY it can report eligible hypothetical economics/health (SAFETY_VALID for proposal routing) while separately setting submission_allowed=false and NOT_SUBMITTABLE_BY_MODE; this is never a dispatch PASS. On final transaction the coordinator verifies agent+account revisions, permission epoch and all scopes, reserves capacity and writes intent/audit/outbox. Dispatcher rechecks immutable terms, expiry, permissions and fresh bounded risk/health evidence; revocation/kill switch increments permission epoch and invalidates pending dispatch leases. Race between a last check and external acceptance cannot be eliminated with a database transaction: mark unknown outcome, cancel/reconcile and never pretend a lock prevented an already transmitted fill.

Exit checks differ from entry checks: confirmed owned quantity, correct environment/account, no over-close, safe protection sequencing, audit and appropriate authority always apply. Entry-only RR, daily entry count, loss lock or unavailable AI must not trap an existing position. Stale prices/connection can still make an exit uncertain; use the preauthorized controlled-reduction policy and record failures. A stop amendment cannot widen risk; a reduction cannot become an entry. See [[01 - Architecture/Execution/Trade Management]] and [[07 - Operations/Runbooks/Incident and Failure Procedures]].

Independent AGENT locks do not automatically freeze other independent accounts. Shared ACCOUNT/SYSTEM integrity locks propagate to every affected agent. Agents cannot remove their own locks; only the trusted supervisor's defined daily reset or authorized administrative recovery can do so. Normal or experimental automation never changes this boundary.
