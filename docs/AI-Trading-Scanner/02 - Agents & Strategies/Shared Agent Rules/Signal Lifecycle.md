# Signal and proposal lifecycle

Phase 4 implements immutable `StrategyDecision` and `TradeProposal` content contracts. Phase 5 adds immutable `SizingDecision`, `RiskDecision` and `CapitalReservation` contracts. Human consent and OrderIntent remain separate. `APPROVED_FOR_RESERVATION` is not user approval or execution approval, and no proposal becomes OPEN without a future fill. Records bind agent/config/input lineage and allocation ownership; shared candidate data does not share decisions or capital.

One immutable proposal may create at most one reservation in the in-memory coordinator, across allocations and parent accounts. A same-owner replay returns that active reservation only after atomically rechecking time, locks, authority and current loss state. At deadline equality or later, replay expires the hold and rejects. RELEASED, CONSUMED and EXPIRED are terminal; repeating the same terminal transition is idempotent, while a conflicting transition fails. RELEASED/EXPIRED restore exact available capital; CONSUMED before expiry moves active reservation to the Phase 5 committed-capital placeholder without claiming an order or position and conservatively retains modeled downside. Post-expiry consume fails closed because Phase 5 has no durable prior-dispatch/fill evidence. A terminal proposal cannot be reserved again.

Every Phase 4 evaluation returns exactly `NO_TRADE` or `TRADE_PROPOSAL`; absence/null never means no trade. Strategy non-trade reasons remain distinct from future `BLOCKED_BY_RISK`, `REJECTED_BY_USER`, expiry and broker rejection events. A proposal contains long-side intent, completed-bar entry reference, future-risk-engine sizing intent with no final quantity, protective stop, strategy objective, management mandate, expected gross/cost/net returns, evidence, causal lineage, validity and authority context. Authority reuses the existing `ExecutionDimensions` contract, so data/run mode, execution environment, submission mode, approval policy, operating context and any experiment identity/type remain separate and hash-bound. The sizing contract can represent a later positive final quantity, and that change alters proposal identity and requires approval against the new content. Phase 4 never populates it. A proposal is not approved, reserved, executable, filled or a position.

Signal flow: OBSERVED → CANDIDATE → STRATEGY_VALID, with REJECTED, EXPIRED, INVALIDATED or CANCELLED paths and all predicates retained. Selection/AI links to the same candidate and cannot reverse strategy rejection. Canonical downstream flow in [[01 - Architecture/Execution/Approval Workflow]] is RISK_VALID → SAFETY_VALID → AWAITING_APPROVAL → USER_APPROVED → REVALIDATING → ORDER_INTENT; legitimate automatic modes use AUTO_AUTHORIZED. SIGNAL_ONLY ends at RECORDED_SIGNAL_ONLY. Distinguish BLOCKED_BY_RISK, BLOCKED_BY_SAFETY and REJECTED_BY_USER. Retire ambiguous APPROVED in new schemas; historical risk approvals never imply human consent.

After intent creation, invalidation/cancellation uses [[01 - Architecture/Execution/Order Lifecycle]], never deletes fills. Display PARTIALLY_FILLED/OPEN/EXITED derives from order/position facts. One proposal version permits at most one entry intent; one signal key permits at most one executing entry across its versions. New versions invalidate unsent predecessors. Dedup key includes agent, allocation, participant/run, strategy version, instrument, trigger interval and direction. Data corrections append evaluations without duplicate trades.

Phase 4 records the strategy candidate ceiling as trigger_end + two primary intervals. Future risk/approval layers must shorten this to min(candidate ceiling, configured entry cutoff, approval TTL); they may never extend it. A 10:05 trigger may first execute at 10:10 before 10:15 candidate expiry. Fresh snapshot/risk checks do not extend expiry; now >= expires_at blocks consent/dispatch. Waiting proposals hold no capital; potentially submitted intents retain reservations until terminal reconciliation.

Transitions append actor/scope/reason/time/expected revision. Repeated events are idempotent. Rejected/expired/unfilled proposals consume zero daily entries; first positive fill consumes one, further partials do not. Non-trades remain analyzable under [[03 - Experiments/Counterfactual Analysis]] without changing actual equity.
