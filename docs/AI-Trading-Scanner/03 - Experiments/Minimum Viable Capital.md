# Minimum Viable Capital and capital efficiency

Status: **metric contract planned; thresholds intentionally unset**.

Minimum Viable Capital (MVC) is the lowest tested capital at which the named strategy/version appears economically viable after realistic costs and risk constraints across the preregistered evidence. It is not the smallest arm with positive observed P&L.

MVC inputs include Net Trading P&L, net return, maximum drawdown, economically executable trade count, cost-driven opportunity rejection rate, total execution costs, execution costs/gross profit, execution costs/capital, sample size and stability across test periods. Preserve unavailable and statistically weak inputs; do not force a classification. MVC criteria and method version must be registered later without rewriting prior results.

Capital efficiency compares net return, absolute Net Trading P&L, Net Economic P&L, maximum drawdown percentage, profit factor, execution costs/capital, execution costs/gross profit, risk-adjusted results when meaningful and cost-driven rejection rate. A capital-efficient range balances return, risk, cost efficiency and capital required. It cannot be inferred from capital size alone.

Fee minima, fractional capability and quantity rounding may make small arms infeasible. That is an experiment result, not a reason to weaken cost assumptions. Capital and AI API budget remain separate ledgers.
