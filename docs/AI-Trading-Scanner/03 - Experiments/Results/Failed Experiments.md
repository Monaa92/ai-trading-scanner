# Failed and inconclusive experiments

No experiments have been run. This note intentionally records that absence; it does not invent failures or performance.

Retain every completed negative, zero-trade, rejected, aborted and technically invalid run. Distinguish:

- **Negative research result:** valid experiment failed its hypothesis/guardrails; retain metrics and rejection rationale.
- **Inconclusive:** uncertainty/coverage insufficient; decision EXPERIMENTAL, no edge claim.
- **Invalid/failed execution:** causality, data, accounting or infrastructure failure; retain artifacts, flag metrics ineligible, diagnose without treating invalid P&L as evidence.
- **Aborted:** stopping rule or operator action with timestamp/reason; partial favorable results do not qualify as completed evidence.

Required future record: registration/run IDs, versions/manifests, original hypothesis, status and failure category, period/data, full result/error artifacts, number of other related trials, diagnosis, decision, corrective proposal and linked superseding run. A rerun gets a new ID. Never overwrite an unfavorable run with a successful rerun or quietly remove a failed symbol/date. See [[03 - Experiments/Experiment Framework]].
